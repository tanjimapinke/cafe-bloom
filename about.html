Our story

How it began

How Café Bloom began

Café Bloom started in 2016 with two friends, Amina and Rafiq, who shared a single idea: that coffee tastes better when it's made for someone you know. They opened a four-table shopfront on a quiet Dhaka side street, roasting small batches of beans on weekends and baking bread before sunrise.

What began as a hobby between two coffee lovers slowly became a neighbourhood habit. Regulars started arriving before the sign was even lit, and by the second year, the four tables had turned into fourteen. The recipe never changed — good ingredients, unhurried mornings, and a seat for anyone who needed one.

Today, Café Bloom is still run with that same spirit: a space built one relationship at a time, cup by cup.

Our Philosophy
