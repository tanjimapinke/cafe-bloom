Our Story

More than just coffee

Café Bloom began as a small corner counter with two chairs and a single espresso machine. Over the years it grew into a gathering place — where regulars know each other's names and every visit feels a little like coming home.

Today we still roast in small batches, cook from scratch each morning, and treat every guest like they've just walked into a friend's kitchen.
